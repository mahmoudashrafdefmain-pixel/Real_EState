2. Decline Triggers & Banking Rationale

  • Underage (Age < 21): Declined because applicant is legally a minor under banking credit laws.
  • Low Income (Income < EGP 20,000): Declined because applicant falls below the solvency baseline.
  • Insufficient Cash (Cash < 50% Income): Declined due to inadequate liquid cash reserves.
  • Comfortable Payment < 60% Income: Declined because payment capacity fails minimum simulation standard.
  • Property Installment > 30% Income: Unit marked OVER BUDGET because installment breaches the 30% DTI ceiling.

3. Capacity & Pricing Formulas

• Max Monthly Installment: Monthly Net Income × 30%
• Approved Loan Capacity: Present Value (PV) of max installment over 15 or 20 years at simulated dynamic rate.
• Max Affordable Property Price: Approved Loan Capacity + Available Cash Savings
• Required Down Payment: Property Price × 20%

📈 Section E: Charts & Statistical Analysis

Median vs. Mean: In Egyptian real estate, the median price (EGP 4.80M) is far more dependable than the mean (EGP 10.46M), which is inflated by multi-hundred-million-pound luxury mansions. Always use the median for typical benchmarking.

Outliers: Properties in the extreme top 1% (above 75M EGP) reflect unique compound exclusivity, prime golf/lake frontage, or land expansion rather than basic housing price inflation.

Environmental Metrics: US AQI measures air particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10); lower is cleaner (0–50 Good, 51–100 Moderate). Green Space % measures satellite NDVI vegetation within 1km radius.

🤖 Section F: Predictive Model

Prediction Target: The machine learning model predicts Price_Numeric (Current Market Asking Price in EGP) using hedonic regression on natural logarithm ln(price).

Zero Leakage Guarantee: The model relies strictly on physical property features (Area, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Floor Level), architectural traits (Class Tier, View, Furnished), and geographic variables (City, District, AQI, Green Space). It never accepts price or derived price ratios as inputs.

Validated Performance (Held-Out Test Data, N = 6,681): R² = 63.9%, Median Absolute Error = EGP 863,237, Mean Absolute Percentage Error = 24.5%, 5-Fold Cross-Validation Mean R² = 72.4% ± 5.3%.

Prediction Uncertainty: Point estimates are accompanied by empirical 80% prediction intervals: [Point Estimate × 0.687, Point Estimate × 1.443]. Predictions are statistical approximations, not guaranteed appraisals.

💼 Section G: Investment Feasibility Lab

Slide 4 Underwriting: Simulates a 3-year off-plan delivery holding period with 2-year construction handover milestone, compounded Net Operating Income (NOI), exit terminal values, and debt amortizations.

Core Metrics: (1) MOIC (Equity Multiple) = Total cash received / Initial cash invested; (2) CAGR = (MOIC)^(1/3) - 1; (3) Leveraged ROI = Net equity return over initial down payment stake.

Two-Way Sensitivity Matrix: Cross-tabulates Exit Cap Rates (6%–10%) vs. Annual Rental Growth (4%–10%) to stress-test returns against adverse market scenarios.

⚠️ Section H: Understanding Results & Limitations

Asking vs. Closed Price Gap: The data reflects portal asking prices. In the Egyptian market, completed transactions typically clear at a 5% to 10% negotiation discount below asking price.

Correlation vs. Causation: Gated compounds command a +454% median price premium over standalone units, driven by security, integrated infrastructure, and developer reputation.

Macro Conditions: High inflation and foreign exchange shifts alter replacement costs. Models reflect current conditions and require periodic recalibration.
